# Internet Access During Restrictions

**Version:** 0.0.1 *(document metadata)* · **Revised:** 2026-10-15 · **Status:** Personal observations

These notes describe the author's experience in Tehran and are not an independent assessment of internet restrictions or VPN services.

There is a recurring pattern in my country that I want to avoid, and one part of it frustrates me. I live in Tehran, Iran. For the past two years, many wars and battles have taken place, and the government has started or been involved in many of them. During these periods, the government has repeatedly restricted public internet access so severely that it becomes an intranet. I am not sure whether “intranet” is the technically accurate term in this context.

Many unreliable VPN vendors claim that their service will work through the strictest government filters. I have tried at least six to eight vendors, and none of the ones I tried worked for me.
